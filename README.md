# Your Digital Coach

a "Tiger Mom" app
a digital tiger mom will motivate you.
at app onboarding, it will ask what youre working on, e.g.: learning an instrument, eating healthy, getting a promotion at work, etc.
the tiger mom will motivate you throughout the day with silly, somewhat sarcastic jibes.
 she would give ideas for your goal
it is important that she not give unhealthy information, like if the user wants to lose weight that is sometimes recommended and sometimes not.
at onboarding users should get a limited number of options for the appearance of their adopted tiger mom
the tiger mom should be illustrated in a number of poses and emotions like happy with you and sad and upset and proud
ideally, there would be functionality where it can see something like you spent 2 hours on
